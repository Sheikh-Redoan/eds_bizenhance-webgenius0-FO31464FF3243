Grammar Discovery

Notice that the stressed Ё in прошёл sounds like O after Ш rather than the usual YO-like sound.

Grammar Discovery

Прошёл is a masculine singular past-tense form.

When addressing a woman informally, the corresponding feminine form is:

Прошла.

This is an important feature of Russian grammar: past-tense verbs can change according to the gender and number of the subject.
